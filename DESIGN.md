# HOLARD public site design

## North star

Present HOLARD as a UAE workforce partner that helps employers staff real sites and helps candidates find work. The visual reference is an operations briefing: confident navy surfaces, clear orange action cues, documentary photography, and generous spacing that keeps service details easy to scan.

## Audience and register

- Primary audiences: UAE employers looking for workforce support and candidates applying for jobs.
- Register: public brand and content site, with separate authenticated HR, client, employee, and investor tools.
- Primary public action: request workers or apply for a role.
- Language: plain English, sentence case, UAE terminology where useful.

## Type and layout

- Display and section headings use Poppins; paragraphs and form labels use Inter.
- Public pages use a centered 1280px content container and a 20px mobile gutter, widening to 32px at tablet widths.
- Section spacing is 64px on mobile and 96px on wide screens.
- Keep service and recruiting content in short sections, cards, and ordered steps so employers can compare and act quickly.
- Mobile actions should be full-width when paired and remain at least 44px tall.

## Shape, imagery, and motion

- Buttons use an 8px radius; content cards use a 16px radius; hero media may use larger rounded panels.
- Use client-approved, original workforce photography. Do not substitute compressed social-media images or add logos/watermarks to photos.
- Orange is reserved for primary actions and key evidence; do not use decorative gradients as a substitute for real photography.
- Keep motion restrained, preserve visible keyboard focus, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Durable content decisions

- The hero should say what HOLARD does, where it operates, and which employers it serves.
- Show the two core actions: hire talent and find a job.
- Trust facts must be traceable to approved company information. Compliance wording should describe support unless an approved certification is supplied.
- Contact numbers, client logos, testimonials, and performance claims require client-provided, approved values before publication.
- New photography remains pending until original source files are supplied.
